Your sugar limit

What counts against my limit?

Added sugar: the sugar and syrups put into food and drinks, like soda, desserts, and sweetened yogurt. Natural sugar in whole fruit, vegetables, and plain milk is shown as total sugar but doesn't count against your limit.

How do I change my limit?

Open Settings in the app and pick a new daily limit, or enter your own. Past days keep the limit that was in place on that day, so changing it never rewrites your streak.

Why does the app suggest a lower limit?

When you stay under your limit on most of the last 7 days, SugarScout offers to lower it by 5g, never below the recommended adult limit. You can always say not now.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row you stayed under your limit. A day you haven't logged yet doesn't break it; a day over your limit starts a fresh one tomorrow.

Scanning

How accurate is AI meal scanning?

It's an estimate based on what's visible in the photo and a typical recipe. You can review every food, change portions, or swap a food before anything is logged. For packaged foods, the barcode scanner uses the label's data.

The barcode scanner didn't find my food.

Some products aren't in the database yet. Tap "Enter the label" to add it from the nutrition label in a few seconds.

Subscription

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose SugarScout. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I already subscribed but the app is asking me to pay.

Tap Restore at the bottom of the subscription screen while signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my food log stored?

On your phone. There's no account. Photos you scan are sent to our server to estimate sugar and are not stored. Full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Your log lives on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. You can also clear everything in Settings with Reset All Data. To ask us to delete anything tied to your anonymous identifiers, email the address above.
