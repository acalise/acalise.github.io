How it works

Prop your phone. Play. Read the report.

No sensors, no paddle attachments, no coach standing over you. Just your phone and a game you were going to play anyway.

1

Film a game

Phone in a corner of the court, about five feet up, on the 0.5x lens, with all four corners in frame. Up to 20 minutes per video.

2

Get your report

A game score plus the habits that cost you the most points, each with a plain explanation of why it matters. In doubles, get one for you or for you and your partner.

Drill what it found

Ranked drills for the exact habits in your report, solo, with a partner, or with a ball machine. Then film your next game and see what changed.

Features

A coach that watches every game.

Doubles team reports

50 drills

Worth being clear about

What Coach Dink is not.

Being specific about the limits makes the reports more useful, because you know how to read them.

It coaches from video. That has boundaries.

  • The rating is Coach Dink's own estimate for tracking progress. It is not an official rating from any pickleball organization.
  • It can only coach what the camera sees. If the court corners are cropped or the phone moves, the report says so and has less to say.
  • It does not replace a good in-person coach. It is the reps between lessons, with eyes on every game.
Questions

Before you download.

What does Coach Dink actually do?

You film a game of pickleball on your iPhone and Coach Dink watches it. A few minutes later you get a report: a game score, the habits that cost you the most points, why each one matters, and the drills that fix it, ranked so you know what to work on first.

Where do I put my phone?

In a corner of the court, about five feet up, on the ultra-wide (0.5x) lens, with all four corners of the court and the server behind the baseline in frame. A tripod, a fence clamp, or a bag on a bench all work. Once it is recording, nobody touches it.

Does it work for doubles?

Yes. Pick yourself, then choose to get a report on just you, or on you and your partner as a team, so you can see how the two of you play together.

Is the rating an official pickleball rating?

No. It is Coach Dink's own estimate for tracking your progress, not an official rating from any pickleball organization. It only moves when you film a game, never from drills alone.

How long can a video be?

Up to 20 minutes per video. One full game is ideal.
