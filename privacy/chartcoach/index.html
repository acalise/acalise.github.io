ChartCoach Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Overview

ChartCoach ("we", "the app") is an iOS app that analyzes screenshots and photos of trading charts and returns educational technical analysis tailored to how you trade. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

No Account Required

ChartCoach does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Data Storage

Your data — your trading profile (the answers you give during setup, such as your experience level, the markets you trade, your style, and the indicators you use), your analysis history, the chart images you analyzed, and your settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a database of your activity. Your data never leaves your device except as described below (AI chart analysis, purchase verification, and advertising attribution).

Camera and Photos

ChartCoach requests camera access only if you choose to photograph a chart on another screen. Photo library access uses Apple's system photo picker, which lets you select a single image without giving the app access to your library. Images you choose are saved locally with the analysis so you can revisit them in History.

AI Chart Analysis

When you submit a chart, the image and your trading profile (never your name or any contact details) are sent to our backend service for analysis. The backend uses Google Gemini to read the chart and generate the analysis. After processing:

  • The image is not stored on our servers beyond the duration of the request.
  • The AI provider does not retain your image for training purposes (per our service agreements).
  • The analysis is returned to your device and stored locally in your history.
  • Requests are tied to a random, anonymous device identifier used only for abuse prevention and rate limiting.

Device Integrity

To protect our service from abuse, ChartCoach uses Apple's App Attest, which lets our backend confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine Apple device. App Attest does not identify you personally and does not track you across apps.

Subscriptions and Purchases

ChartCoach requires an auto-renewable subscription. All purchases and billing are handled entirely through Apple's App Store; we do not collect or store any payment information. Subscription status is verified through RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your entitlement status; no name, email address, phone number, or payment details are shared by ChartCoach. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles purchases.

No Ads Inside the App

ChartCoach does not display any ads and does not show you third-party advertising while you use it. We may run ads for ChartCoach on other platforms to reach new users, and we measure how those ads perform as described below.

Attribution and Advertising

We may run ads for ChartCoach on other platforms, including Apple Search Ads. For Apple Search Ads campaigns we use Apple's AdServices attribution, which does not identify you across other companies' apps or websites. Installs from other platforms are measured using Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork. ChartCoach does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Install data may be shared with these advertising partners solely to measure the performance of our own ads. We do not sell your data.

Third-Party Services

ChartCoach uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini — for analyzing the chart images you submit (images are sent for analysis and not stored)
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management (an anonymous app user ID and entitlement status are shared)
  • Apple (App Attest, Search Ads, and SKAdNetwork) — for device integrity and privacy-preserving measurement of our own advertising

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with third parties only as described above: with Google Gemini for AI chart analysis, with RevenueCat for subscription management, and with Apple's advertising measurement services solely to measure the performance of our own ads.

Children's Privacy

ChartCoach is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting individual analyses from History or by deleting the app. Anonymous records held by our service providers (RevenueCat) are retained in accordance with each provider's data retention policy.

Not Financial Advice

ChartCoach is for informational and educational purposes only and does not provide financial or investment advice. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security or asset.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
